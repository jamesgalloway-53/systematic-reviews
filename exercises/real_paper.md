# Insecticide-treated bed nets and child mortality: a randomised trial
**Journal:** Global Child Health (illustrative). 
## Abstract
**Background.** Malaria is a leading cause of death in children under five.
**Methods.** In this cluster-randomised trial, 40 villages (about 3,400 children
under five) were randomised to receive insecticide-treated bed nets (ITNs) or no
nets. The primary outcome was all-cause mortality over 18 months.
**Results.** All-cause mortality was 34 deaths per 1,000 child-years in the ITN
group and 51 per 1,000 child-years in the control group.
**Conclusion.** ITNs substantially reduced child mortality.
## Results table

|---|---|---|
| Deaths | 58 | 87 |
