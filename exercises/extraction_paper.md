# Oral zinc for acute diarrhoea in children: a randomised controlled trial
**Journal:** Journal of Child Nutrition (illustrative).
## Methods
Children aged 6–59 months with acute diarrhoea were randomised to oral zinc or
placebo for 14 days. Primary outcome: diarrhoea persisting beyond 7 days.
## Results
Of 220 children randomised (110 per arm), diarrhoea persisted beyond 7 days in
18 of 110 children in the zinc group and 31 of 110 in the placebo group.
Secondary outcome — hospital admission — occurred in 4/110 (zinc) and 9/110 (placebo).
| Outcome | Zinc (n=110) | Placebo (n=110) |
|---|---|---|
| Persistent diarrhoea (>7 days) | 18 | 31 |
| Hospital admission | 4 | 9 |
| Any vomiting | 22 | 19 |
## Funding
Illustrative example for teaching; not a real trial.
