# Pod 2 — Title & abstract screening set
Topic: insecticide-treated bed nets (ITNs) and child mortality / malaria.
Screen each: Include / Exclude / Unsure, with a one-line reason.

## 1
RCT of ITNs vs no nets in 2,000 children under 5 in a malaria-endemic district.
Outcome: all-cause mortality over 24 months. (Directly on topic — RCT, right population/outcome.)

## 2
Narrative review discussing the history of malaria control policy in West Africa.
No original data, no defined outcome. (Review, not a primary study.)

## 3
Cluster-RCT comparing ITNs vs untreated nets; outcome: clinical malaria episodes
in children aged 6–59 months. (On topic — cluster RCT, relevant comparator.)

## 4
Laboratory study of pyrethroid resistance mechanisms in Anopheles gambiae mosquitoes.
No human participants. (Basic science — outside PICO.)

## 5
Cohort study of ITN ownership and under-5 mortality using national survey data.
(Observational but on topic — decide per your inclusion rule on design.)

## 6
RCT of a malaria vaccine (RTS,S) vs placebo in infants; outcome: severe malaria.
(Different intervention — vaccine, not nets.)

## 7
Before-and-after evaluation of a district ITN distribution campaign; outcome: parasite
prevalence in children. (On topic but weak design — Unsure, depends on inclusion rule.)

## 8
Qualitative study of why families do or do not sleep under bed nets.
(Qualitative — include only if your review covers barriers/uptake.)

## 9
RCT of ITNs plus indoor residual spraying vs ITNs alone; outcome: child mortality.
(On topic — relevant if combination interventions are in scope.)

## 10
Editorial arguing that donor funding for bed nets should increase.
(Opinion piece — no data.)
